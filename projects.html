Network installation

Small-business office network

Location
Solwezi District
Problem
Desks on a tangle of extension leads and a home router, so the internet drops whenever someone prints.
Solution
Every desk cabled to a labelled patch panel, one managed switch, business Wi-Fi with a separate guest network, and a drawing left with the owner.
Equipment
Cat6 structured cabling, 24-port managed switch, business access points
Result
A network the owner can explain to the next technician.
